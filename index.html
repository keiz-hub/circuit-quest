How to Play

Move to the active gate

Desktop Move using WASD or Arrow Keys. Press Space or click Interact only when you are beside a gate or console.
Mobile Drag the joystick to move your character. Move beside a gate or console, then tap the Interact button beside the joystick.

Tip: Gates are not clickable. Walk beside the highlighted gate first, then press Interact. The exit opens only after the mission is completed.
